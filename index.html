How It Works

  1. 01
    You enter twelve values. Age, gender, country, academic level, the platform you use most, why you use it, how many hours you spend on it daily, how often you unlock your phone, your study hours, physical activity, sleep, and self-reported stress.
  2. 02
    The data is sent to a FastAPI server. Your inputs are encoded into a JSON payload and posted to the /predict endpoint. The server validates every field against the same constraints enforced in the form.
  3. 03
    A trained model returns a score. The backend passes your data through a scikit-learn pipeline trained on the Student Social Media & Mental Health dataset. The score is a continuous value representing predicted depression risk.
  4. 04
    The result is displayed alongside your profile. The /get endpoint retrieves the recorded result. Both the score and the exact inputs the model saw are shown so you can verify the submission was correct.
Important. This tool uses a statistical model trained on survey data. It is not a substitute for professional mental health evaluation. If you are experiencing distress, speak to a qualified counsellor or clinician.
